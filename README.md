### 🙋🏾‍♂️ About me

- 📱 I build mobile apps, mostly **Kotlin + Jetpack Compose** these days, with a growing love for **Kotlin Multiplatform**, and a history of shipping with **Flutter**.
- 🌱 Currently learning **Kotlin on the backend with Spring Boot**.
- 😄 Pronouns: He/Him.
- 👯 Always happy to collaborate on mobile apps and open source.
- 🎯 Looking for opportunities to keep growing as a mobile developer, so don't hesitate to reach out.
- 💬 Ask me about anything: Android architecture, Compose, Firebase, Kotlin on the server, or what I'm reading.
- ⚡ Fun fact: when I'm not building apps or squashing bugs, I'm playing a mind-puzzling game, enjoying art & music, or deep in a book or podcast.
